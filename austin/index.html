Keeping Your Austin HVAC System Running Longer

Routine maintenance makes all the difference between a system that lasts and one that quits in the middle of a heat wave. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a gentle rinse each spring for the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so small issues get caught early.

Take a look at the age of your system. Its age tells you a lot. With regular service, most air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that point and is also showing rising bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or repeated repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency replacement in peak season.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor ignored for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.

Typical Heating & Cooling Issues in Austin

HVAC systems in this area face a familiar set of enemies. Long cooling seasons push compressors and capacitors to their limits, and salt air and moisture chew at outdoor condenser coils and fins, cutting efficiency years before the equipment should show its age. Dirty filters quietly choke off airflow, freezing evaporator coils in summer and overheating furnace heat exchangers in winter, damage that builds slowly and out of sight.

Our technicians also run into the full range of component failures in local homes: capacitors that leave the outdoor unit humming but not starting, contactors with pitted points, cracked igniters and dirty flame sensors that lock furnaces out on cold mornings, slow refrigerant leaks that leave the house lukewarm by afternoon, and blower motors worn down by years of restricted airflow. The area's older housing stock adds its own layer, undersized returns, leaky or crushed ductwork, and additions that no duct run ever reached.

Every one of these problems is fixable, and most are much less expensive when caught early. If your system is doing anything unusual, new noises, weak airflow, rooms that won't hold temperature, bills creeping upward, a quick diagnostic visit from our team catches the small problem before it becomes a costly breakdown.
