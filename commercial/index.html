Restaurant Services

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

Hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort all interact, and a system that's undersized or failing shows up first as a sweltering cook line and complaints at the tables. Fryers, ranges, and grills dump huge heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air straight out of the building. Our food-service work covers rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Many restaurant comfort complaints trace back to air balance. When a kitchen runs under negative pressure, it pulls dining-room air the wrong way, sucks hot or cold outdoor air in through every door, and wears out your staff during the rush. Our technicians balance makeup air against hood exhaust and document every visit in written reports that landlords and auditors can review.

Your schedule comes first. We coordinate every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. Off-hours installation is available when needed, and disruption stays minimal so the kitchen keeps cooking and the dining room stays comfortable.

Inspections & Documentation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Badly installed or neglected HVAC equipment can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. That's a real liability for any commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Our commercial installations and inspections cover heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Fast Response

Fast Commercial HVAC Service

When a commercial HVAC system fails, the clock starts immediately. Kitchens overheat, retail floors empty out, server rooms heat up, and tenants start calling. Calls to our commercial line get an experienced technician dispatched fast, throughout Texas City and the surrounding area.

Commercial emergencies come in many forms. It might be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, an ignition or gas valve fault, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board lockout. Whatever it is, we find it fast, get the building comfortable again, and record every step for your service records. Afterward, we put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again.

Preventive Care

Proactive HVAC Care for Businesses

Preventing failures is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment. Every Texas City HVAC program is customized to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and includes scheduled inspections, coil cleaning, condensate line clearing, filter changes using the right MERV rating for the space, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal preparation before peak demand.

Compared with an emergency replacement, scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction as much, and it sharply cuts the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Clients who switch to a program usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills. Give us a call to discuss a plan for your property and budget.
