Upfront Pricing

Clear Pricing Before Any Work Starts

Effective HVAC service starts with knowing exactly what you're dealing with. Texas City HVAC offers honest, upfront pricing throughout Texas City, handling everything from a worn capacitor to a full system replacement. Every job begins with a careful inspection of your equipment, including its age, tonnage, fuel type, refrigerant charge, and duct condition, followed by a plain-English recommendation with the price spelled out before any work starts.

Many HVAC problems stay hidden until a failure is well underway. Refrigerant seeps out through pinhole leaks in the evaporator coil, blower bearings wear quietly, and a weakening capacitor gives no warning until the compressor won't start on the first hot day. If you notice weak airflow, rising energy bills, ice on the refrigerant lines, or rooms that never reach the thermostat setting, schedule an inspection sooner rather than later. Catching it early usually keeps a repairable issue from becoming a replacement.

We also help homeowners plan ahead. Most air conditioners and furnaces last 12 to 18 years, and a planned replacement is nearly always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency swap during a heat wave or cold snap. Our technicians know the climate demands common to Texas City and how they affect equipment life, so we can tell you honestly whether your system has years left or is living on borrowed time.

Seasonal Tune-Ups

Seasonal HVAC Maintenance Plans

Preventing a breakdown beats paying for one. That's the idea behind our maintenance plans. On each scheduled visit, we clean coils and burners, check the refrigerant charge, test the electrical components, inspect the heat exchanger, flush the condensate drain, and swap in a new filter, all tailored to the equipment in your home.

We offer flexible maintenance options for homeowners, with spring cooling tune-ups and fall heating tune-ups for most systems. This is especially important in dusty areas, where dirty condenser coils choke heat rejection and drive up energy use. Each home is assessed individually, and the plan is tailored to your equipment's age, your household's comfort demands, and your local climate.

The savings show up quickly. Clean coils move heat with less effort, a correct refrigerant charge takes strain off the compressor, and a fresh filter spares the blower motor. Compared with the cost of replacing a system even one year early, a maintenance plan is a bargain.

Proper Sizing

The Right Size System for Your Home

An undersized system runs nonstop and never catches up on the hottest days, while an oversized one short-cycles, wastes energy, and leaves the house humid. Texas City HVAC sizes every replacement and new installation to your actual home. That means a proper load calculation that accounts for square footage, insulation, window exposure, and duct layout, rather than simply matching the tonnage of the old unit. For ductless mini-splits, it means matching head capacity to each zone so every room holds its setpoint.

Houses change over time. An addition or a finished basement can outgrow the single-stage system that used to be plenty. Modern two-stage and variable-speed heat pumps hold tighter temperatures and use much less energy. If you're torn between a heat pump and a furnace-and-AC pair, or ducted and ductless, we'll lay out the real trade-offs in SEER2 ratings, operating cost, electrical and venting needs, and installation work.

Repairs We Handle

Heating and Cooling Problems We Solve in Texas City

Homes in Texas City run into a familiar set of heating and cooling failures, and each one calls for a different fix. Texas City HVAC handles them all: no cooling, air that blows but never gets cold, furnaces that won't ignite or short-cycle, frozen evaporator coils, refrigerant leaks, condensers that hum without starting, thermostats that lose their settings, clogged condensate drains, and mini-splits flashing error codes. Our technicians identify the exact fault and target the repair to the component that actually failed.

Not every HVAC problem is equally urgent. A bad capacitor just keeps the compressor from starting. A cracked heat exchanger, on the other hand, can leak combustion gases into your living space, and a blocked condensate drain can quietly ruin a ceiling before anyone notices. Our technicians carry the parts to replace capacitors, contactors, fan motors, igniters, flame sensors, and control boards, seal ductwork that's leaking air into the attic, and recharge refrigerant once a leak has been repaired.
