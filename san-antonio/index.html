Heating and Air Conditioning Service Across San Antonio

Heating and cooling around here covers a lot of ground: aging furnaces in established neighborhoods, high-efficiency heat pumps in recent construction, ductless mini-splits in additions and converted garages, and packaged units on commercial buildings. Our team handles all of it. We have years of field experience, and we know how the local climate, the housing stock, and local code affect the way a system performs and fails.

A typical week for our residential team might include capacitor and contactor replacements, flame sensor cleanings, refrigerant leak searches, blower motor swaps, plus the occasional full system replacement or ductless addition. Two situations call for quick action from you before a technician arrives. The first is an iced-over air conditioner: turn the system off at the thermostat and set the fan to "on" so the moving air thaws the coil, because running it frozen can overflow the drain pan and hurt the compressor. The second is a furnace that keeps cycling on and off or produces a burning smell. Shut it down at the switch and give us a call. Those few minutes can save you from a far bigger repair.

Keeping Your San Antonio HVAC System Running Longer

Most mid-season breakdowns trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Change your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

How old is your system? That number says a lot about what comes next. With regular service, most air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, gas furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that point and is also showing higher bills, uneven temperatures, or frequent repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than a rushed emergency install in peak season.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor left alone for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.

Common HVAC Problems in San Antonio

Most local HVAC failures announce themselves before they happen. A breaker that trips more than once, a condenser that hums louder each month, rooms drifting further from the thermostat setting, a musty smell at startup. These are the early symptoms of failing capacitors, refrigerant leaks, dirty coils, and duct problems, and caught early they're a quick visit. Left alone, they take out compressors and heat exchangers, the two most expensive parts in the system. We diagnose the cause, not just the symptom, so small problems stay small.
